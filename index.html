Three principles
01
Understand before judging.
We seek understanding before judgment. Every book, every idea deserves a fair hearing first.
02
Question ideas, not people.
We examine assumptions and reasoning — not motives or identities. The argument, not the arguer.
03
Clarity over consensus.
Our goal is clarity, not agreement. We leave with sharper thinking, not necessarily the same conclusions.
About

42 Book Club is a small, curious group that meets to read science fiction and speculative literature — books that ask the questions most people avoid. We named ourselves after the answer to life, the universe, and everything, because we believe the quality of the question matters more than the answer.

"The answer to the great question of life, the universe, and everything is… forty-two."
Douglas Adams, The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy

We read broadly — from Asimov's logical robots to Adams' absurdist cosmos — and meet monthly to discuss not just the book, but the ideas it surfaces. No expertise required. Only curiosity.
